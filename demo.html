This demo embodies the bench: egocentric partial obs + controllable difficulty (size = the OOD axis; landmark density / ambiguity = how much re-anchoring is possible) + full ground truth (position, map, trail, landmarks) for the drift/correction metrics. A model trained here must integrate motion (drifts) and re-anchor on landmarks (corrects), then generalize small→large.
